Segmentation revision (2026-09-16):
- Goal addition: choose audio boundaries and update cadence deliberately to balance draft latency, repeated work, and recognition accuracy.
- [decision] Use non-overlapping utterance speech separated by credible silence, with silence padding and repeated recognition of only the active utterance. Preserve finalized utterance text until full-recording finalization. Do not use text overlap guessing or require timestamps that text-only backends cannot supply.
- [decision] Use a soft duration target: shorten required silence for long utterances, but never cut at a fixed time through uninterrupted speech. Reduce update frequency according to measured inference cost and active duration. Keep existing enhancement/gain consistent for silence analysis; model parameters remain unchanged.
- [decision] Endpoint analysis uses energy hysteresis: normal configured VAD energy establishes usable voice, but one-fifth that energy preserves quiet continuation. This is a conservative segmentation heuristic, not a guarantee that energy separates every voice from noise.
- [decision] Initial partial recognition requires 1.2 seconds of context. Later updates require at least 1.5 seconds of new audio, 2.5 times the measured previous inference duration, or 8% of active-window duration, whichever is larger. Endpoints can complete promptly. These are task-specific balancing defaults, not experimentally established universal optima.
- [decision] No hard latency/window guarantee for uninterrupted speech or a slow backend. Spoken lookback and stable-prefix alignment are deferred because they require a stronger backend contract; stopping still reconciles from all original audio.
